Questions before you start?

Do I need a medical background?

The introductions assume no specialist cardiology knowledge. They build vocabulary and concepts; they do not replace formal medical education.

Can I preview the lessons?

Yes. Every lesson is available to read without an account. Student accounts, once activated, add enrollment and saved progress.

Are these video courses?

The current courses use reading lessons, visual illustrations, and knowledge checks. No video library is advertised or included.
