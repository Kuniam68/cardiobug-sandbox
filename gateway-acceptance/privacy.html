Your information

Privacy, explained.

Last updated 16 November 2026. This describes the current implementation and its account setup requirements.

Before account activation

You can read course material without an account. When the authentication provider is not configured, signup and contact delivery are disabled and no account details are collected through those forms.

Student accounts

Once Supabase is configured, the authentication service processes your email, password, and chosen display name. The learning database stores your account identifier, enrolled course identifiers, completed lesson identifiers, and related timestamps. Passwords are handled by the authentication provider rather than placed in course source files.

Session storage

The sign-in session is stored in your browser tab’s session storage. It is used to keep authenticated requests associated with your account. Sign out on shared devices. This site does not add advertising trackers or analytics.

Contact messages

When an academy contact address is configured, the contact form opens a draft in your email app. A message is delivered only when you send it there. Do not include medical records, sensitive health information, or passwords.

Hosting and external services

GitHub Pages hosts this website. Google Fonts and jsDelivr serve fonts and application libraries, including the interactive heart renderer. If a Turnstile site key is configured, Cloudflare processes verification signals on authentication forms. Tokens are sent to Supabase when available. Enforcement remains disabled until the operator enables it in Supabase. These services receive connection information such as your IP address. Configured accounts use Supabase; optional Google sign-in is shown only when enabled. Further-reading links lead to independent websites.

Access and removal

Use the contact page to request account support or removal after the academy’s contact address is activated. The site operator must publish their legal identity, working contact address, retention policy, and any jurisdiction-specific disclosures before opening account registration to the public.

Operator identity, contact details, and a retention period have not yet been supplied. These details and the authentication provider’s settings must be finalized before public account registration.

Payments and enrollment records

Official sandbox checkout is currently unavailable. When activated, billing details will be sent securely from our server to SSLCOMMERZ to create a hosted payment session. Cardiobug will not collect card numbers, CVVs, PINs or banking passwords. We retain order references, exact amounts/currencies, verification outcomes, enrollment entitlements, refund status and audit events. Billing form details are not stored in our payment database or application logs.

Payment history is visible only to its account owner and authorized academy administrators. Your saved lesson and quiz progress remains associated with your account.
