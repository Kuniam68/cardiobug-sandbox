PURCHASE SUPPORT

Refunds & payment help.

Official sandbox checkout is not yet available. Cardiobug is not accepting payments at this time.

Before paid enrollment opens

Course-specific eligibility, refund windows, the academy’s legal business details and a verified support contact will be published before any purchase can be submitted. Until then, no customer payment or refund is processed.

How a payment is confirmed

Course access requires confirmation from the payment provider and server verification of the exact order, amount and currency. A browser return page is not a receipt. Your signed-in dashboard will show your verified payment history.

Payment problems

If a future payment is pending or uncertain, use Check payment status. Avoid paying again until the original order is reconciled. A cancelled or failed payment does not grant course access.

Refund processing

Administrators can prepare an audited full-refund request for a verified payment. Execution requires separate authorization and merchant-provider support. Access is revoked only after a refund is verified; existing learning records are retained.
