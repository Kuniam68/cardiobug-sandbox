Learning responsibly

A clear starting point.

Last updated 16 November 2026.

Educational use

Cardiobug provides free introductory educational material about cardiovascular concepts. It does not provide medical diagnosis, individualized advice, or emergency services.

Course scope

The library may include clearly labeled sample courses alongside published academy content. They consist of written lessons and knowledge checks. Completion does not confer a clinical qualification, professional certification, or continuing education credit. Illustrations are simplified and should not be used as clinical references.

Accounts

Use accurate account information and keep your credentials private. Do not upload patient information or sensitive medical records. Saved progress reflects your learning activity and is not proof of professional competence.

Availability and external resources

Features depend on hosting, network access, and configured third-party services. External resources have their own terms and privacy practices. Protected paid-course infrastructure is available, but official sandbox checkout is closed. No customer payments are accepted until merchant approval, provider acceptance and separate activation authorization are complete.

Questions and corrections

Use the contact page to ask about course material or report a correction once operator contact details are available. The operator must publish its actual business/support details and final purchase/refund terms before accepting payments.
