What you can expect

Focused written lessons, a manageable learning path, and pointers to established public health information. You can preview all lessons without creating an account.

Honest about the scope

This academy currently offers foundational educational material. It is not a university, clinical service, or accredited training provider. The courses do not award professional qualifications or continuing medical education credits.

About the content

The current curriculum is clearly labeled sample material awaiting replacement, with links to further reading from established health organizations. Faculty profiles and clinical-review credentials have not been supplied; no instructor qualifications or endorsements are claimed.

Learning with care

Medical understanding develops with practice, context, and supervision. Do not use a lesson, knowledge check, or heart illustration to diagnose a condition or make treatment decisions. The 3D model is an original anatomical-inspired illustration and is not a validated anatomical reference.
